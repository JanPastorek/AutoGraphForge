### Context and Relevance
*   **Definitions:** 
    *   The **Roman domination number** $\gamma_R(G)$ is the minimum weight of a function $f: V \to \{0, 1, 2\}$ such that every vertex with a label of 0 is adjacent to at least one vertex with a label of 2[3][4].

*   **Research Focus:** Researchers often compare these parameters to find sharp upper or lower bounds for specific classes of graphs (e.g., trees, trapezoid graphs, or grid graphs)[1][2][6].
