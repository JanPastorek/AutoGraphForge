In the seminal paper **"On Roman domination in graphs"** by E.J. Cockayne, P.A. Dreyer Jr., S.M. Hedetniemi, and S.T. Hedetniemi (*Discrete Mathematics*, 2004), the authors provide several structural results relating the Roman domination number $\gamma_R(G)$ to graph parameters such as the radius and diameter[1][2][3].

Specifically, the paper includes the following theorem regarding the radius and diameter for graphs where $\gamma_R(G) = \gamma(G) + 2$:

*   **Theorem 2.18:** If $G$ is a connected graph and $\gamma_R(G) = \gamma(G) + 2$, then $2 \le \text{rad}(G) \le 4$ and $3 \le \text{diam}(G) \le 8$[4].

This result is often cited in subsequent literature when discussing bounds on the Roman domination number in relation to the distance properties of a graph[4].
