The paper **"Bounds on Roman domination numbers of graphs"** by **B.P. Mobaraky** and **S.M. Sheikholeslami** was published in the journal ***Matematički Vesnik***, Volume 60, Issue 4, in **2008** (pages 247–253)[1][2][3].

### Summary of the Paper
*   **Topic:** The authors investigate the Roman domination number ($\gamma_R(G)$) of a graph $G$[1][4].
*   **Key Results:** The paper provides **lower and upper bounds** for the Roman domination number in terms of fundamental graph parameters, specifically the **diameter** and the **girth** of the graph[1][5]. 
*   **Specific Finding:** For example, the paper establishes that if a graph $G$ has a diameter of two, then $\gamma_R(G)$ is bounded by a specific value[6].

This work is frequently cited in subsequent research regarding Roman domination in graph theory[7][8][9], including studies that later improved these bounds for specific classes of graphs (such as graphs with a minimum degree $\delta(G) \geq 2$)[10][5].
