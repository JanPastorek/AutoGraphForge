In broader graph theory research:

### Key Definitions
*   **Radius ($\text{rad}(G)$):** The minimum eccentricity of any vertex in the graph: $\text{rad}(G) = \min_{v \in V} \{ \max_{u \in V} d(v, u) \}$[7][8].
*   **Roman Domination Number ($\gamma_R(G)$):** The minimum weight of a function $f: V \to \{0, 1, 2\}$ such that every vertex with label $0$ is adjacent to at least one vertex with label $2$[9][10].
