The paper you are likely looking for is **"Lower bounds for the total (distance) k-domination number of a graph"** by **Randy Davila**, which was indexed on arXiv in June 2024 (arXiv:2406.08770)[1][2][3].

### Overview of the Paper
*   **Topic:** The paper explores the "total distance $k$-domination number" of a graph, denoted as $\gamma_{t,k}(G)$[3]. A total distance $k$-dominating set is a set $S \subseteq V(G)$ such that every vertex in the graph is within distance $k$ of some vertex in $S$ *other than itself*[3].
*   **Key Contribution:** The author notes that while there are many results for standard distance domination, there has been a relative lack of well-known lower bounds for the *total* $k$-domination variant[4][5]. This work establishes several new lower bounds and demonstrates how existing lower bounds for the total domination number can be generalized to the total distance $k$-domination number[3].
*   **Context:** This research falls under the field of Combinatorics (math.CO)[2][6].
