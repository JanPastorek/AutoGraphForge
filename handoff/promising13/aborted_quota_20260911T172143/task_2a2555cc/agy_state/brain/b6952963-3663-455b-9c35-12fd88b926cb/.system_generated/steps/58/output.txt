### Context and Definitions
*   **$\text{rad}(G)$ (Radius):** The minimum eccentricity of any vertex in the graph, where eccentricity is the maximum distance from a vertex to any other vertex in the graph[1][2][4].
*   **$\gamma_t(G)$ (Total Domination Number):** The minimum cardinality of a set $S \subseteq V(G)$ such that every vertex in the graph (including those in $S$) is adjacent to at least one vertex in $S$[5][6][3]. This is only defined for graphs without isolated vertices[5][3].
