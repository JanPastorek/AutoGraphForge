The inequality relating the total domination number $\gamma_t(G)$ and the radius $\text{rad}(G)$ of a connected graph $G$ is:

$$\gamma_t(G) \ge \text{rad}(G)$$

This result is documented in graph theory literature (e.g., Theorem 2.15 in various texts on total domination) for connected graphs of order at least two[1].
