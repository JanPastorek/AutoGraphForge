The theorem you are referring to is:

**"If $G$ is a connected graph of order at least two, then $\gamma_t(G) \geq \text{rad}(G)$."**[1][2][3]

This result is identified as **Theorem 2.15** in the literature on total domination in graphs (specifically cited in works such as those by DeLaVina et al.[1][2]). In this inequality:
*   $\gamma_t(G)$ denotes the **total domination number** of the graph $G$[4].
*   $\text{rad}(G)$ denotes the **radius** of the graph $G$[2].
