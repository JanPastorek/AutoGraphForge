In graph theory, the relationship between the total domination number, denoted as $\gamma_t(G)$, and the radius of a graph, denoted as $\text{rad}(G)$, is a well-studied topic[1][2][3].

Research has established lower bounds for the total domination number in terms of the radius of the graph[1]. Specifically, it has been noted in the literature that:

$$\gamma_t(G) \ge \text{rad}(G)$$

This inequality appears in studies concerning lower bounds for domination parameters[1]. Furthermore, this radius-based lower bound has been generalized in various contexts, including to the total distance $k$-domination number, $\gamma_{t,k}(G)$[4][1].
