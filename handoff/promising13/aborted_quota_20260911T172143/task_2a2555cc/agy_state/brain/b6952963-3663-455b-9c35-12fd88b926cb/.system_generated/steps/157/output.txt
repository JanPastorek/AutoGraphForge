The research concerning "total domination," "radius," and Randy Davila can be found in the paper titled **"Lower bounds for the total (distance) k-domination number of a graph,"** which is available as an arXiv preprint[1][2][3].

### Key Details:
*   **Topic:** The paper explores lower bounds for the **total distance $k$-domination number** ($\gamma_t^k(G)$) of a graph[1][3].
*   **Radius Bound:** One of the specific contributions is the generalization of a known radius lower bound for the total domination number to the total $k$-domination number[1][2][3]. The authors state that for a connected graph $G$ of order $n \ge 2$ and $k \ge 1$:
    $$\gamma_t^k(G) \ge \frac{1}{k} \text{rad}(G)$$
*   **Context:** This work extends earlier results, such as those by DeLaViña et al., which established bounds for the total domination number (the $k=1$ case)[3].

Randy Davila has authored or co-authored several papers on graph theory invariants, including other studies on distance domination and sub-$k$-domination numbers[4][5][6][7].
